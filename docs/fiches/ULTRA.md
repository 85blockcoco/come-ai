# FICHES ULTRA-CONDENSÉES

## T1
VC = minority early-stage. Power law. Staged financing. Equity Seed 15-30%. IRR Seed 40-70%. Founders <50% Series B.

## T2
PMF, unit economics (CAC/LTV), due diligence (commercial/tech/juridique/financier/RH).

## T3
Post=Pre+Inv. Ownership=Inv/Post. Dilution=old×(1-part). MOIC=sortie/capital. IRR=VAN=0. Anti-dilution: weighted/full ratchet. Liquidation pref: 1x, participating, capped.

## T4
Term sheet = accord de principe. Clauses: valuation, liquidation pref, participation, cap, anti-dilution, vesting, board, veto, drag/tag, ROFR. Convertible note & SAFE.

## T5
Gouvernance: board, veto, reporting. Exits: M&A, IPO, secondary, buyback, write-off. Waterfall. Zombie.
