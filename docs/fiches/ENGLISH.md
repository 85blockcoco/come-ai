# English Fiches — Venture Capital (Detailed)

## T1 — VC & Ecosystem
- VC = minority investment in early-stage startups under uncertainty.
- PE strategies: VC (minority/early), growth equity (minority/mature), buyout (majority), alternative.
- Startup: innovation, scalability, high risk/reward, resource-constrained, iterative (PMF).
- VC distinct: power law; 90–95% fail plan; 70–80% miss return; 20–40+ startups/fund; staged financing; preferred equity.
- Equity by round: Pre-seed 5–15%, Seed 15–30%, A 20–30%, B 15–25%, C 10–20%.
- IRR targets: Seed 40–70%, A 35–50%, B 30–40%, C 25–35%.
- Founders: <50% by Series B, 20–30% at exit.
- Governance: board, veto, mentoring, fundraising.

## T2 — Startup Evaluation
- Opportunity: market size/growth/lifecycle, competition, barriers, timing.
- Team: complementarity, experience, vision, motivation.
- Product/tech: PMF, differentiation, scalability.
- Business model: unit economics, CAC/LTV, margins.
- Financial plan: projections, assumptions, scenarios, milestones.
- Due diligence: commercial, technical, legal, financial, HR.
- Process: screening → evaluation → DD → negotiation → IC → closing.

## T3 — Valuation, Ownership, Returns
- Post = Pre + Invest. Ownership VC = Inv/Post. Price/share = Pre/shares. New shares = Inv/price.
- Dilution: new = old × (1 − share sold).
- MOIC = Exit / Capital. IRR = NPV=0.
- Anti-dilution: weighted average vs full ratchet.
- Liquidation pref: 1x, participating, non-participating, capped.
- Down round: ratchet, pay-to-play, cram-down.
- Returns: IRR targets by stage; MOIC targets; power law.

## T4 — Term Sheets
- Term sheet: non-binding key conditions.
- Clauses: valuation, liquidation pref, participation, cap, anti-dilution, vesting (cliff, acceleration), board, veto, drag/tag, ROFR, co-sale, redemption, pay-to-play, conversion, dividends.
- Convertibles & SAFE: cap, discount, interest, maturity, MFN.
- Negotiation: trade-offs, alignment.

## T5 — Governance, Monitoring, Exits
- Governance: board composition, veto, reporting, information rights.
- Monitoring: KPIs, milestones, reporting.
- Exits: M&A, IPO, secondary, buyback, write-off; timing; waterfall.
- Zombie companies, down rounds.

## Exam Patterns
- Valuation calculations, multi-round cap table, term sheet clauses, down round case, exit waterfall.

## Study Strategy
- Master T3 & T4 first, then T5, T2, T1.
- Practice with 2023–2025 exam solutions.
