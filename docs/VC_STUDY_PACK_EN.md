# VENTURE CAPITAL — FULL STUDY PACK (ENGLISH)

## 1. Core Concepts
- **VC**: minority investment in early-stage startups under high uncertainty, aiming for rapid scaling.
- **PE strategies**: VC (minority/early), growth equity (minority/mature), buyout (majority), alternative (distressed, real assets).
- **Startup**: innovation, scalability, high risk/high reward, resource-constrained, iterative (PMF).
- **VC distinct**: power law returns; 90–95% fail business plan; 70–80% miss projected return; 20–40+ startups/fund; staged financing; preferred equity.
- **Equity by round**: Pre-seed 5–15%, Seed 15–30%, Series A 20–30%, B 15–25%, C 10–20%.
- **Required IRR**: Seed 40–70%, A 35–50%, B 30–40%, C 25–35%.
- **Founders**: <50% by Series B, 20–30% at exit.
- **Governance**: board, veto, mentoring, fundraising.

## 2. Evaluation
- **Opportunity**: market size/growth/lifecycle, competition, barriers, timing.
- **Team**: complementarity, experience, vision, motivation.
- **Product/tech**: PMF, differentiation, scalability.
- **Business model**: unit economics, CAC/LTV, margins.
- **Financial plan**: projections, assumptions, scenarios, milestones.
- **Due diligence**: commercial, technical, legal, financial, HR.
- **Process**: screening → evaluation → DD → negotiation → IC → closing.

## 3. Valuation, Ownership, Returns
- **Post-money** = Pre-money + Investment.
- **VC ownership** = Investment / Post-money.
- **Price per share** = Pre-money / pre shares.
- **New shares** = Investment / price per share.
- **Dilution**: new = old × (1 − share sold).
- **MOIC** = Exit value / Capital invested.
- **IRR**: rate discounting cash flows to NPV=0.
- **Anti-dilution**: weighted average vs full ratchet.
- **Liquidation pref**: 1x, participating, non-participating, capped.
- **Down round**: ratchet, pay-to-play, cram-down.
- **Returns**: IRR targets by stage; MOIC targets; power law.

## 4. Term Sheets
- **Term sheet**: non-binding document of key conditions.
- **Clauses**: valuation, liquidation pref, participation, cap, anti-dilution, vesting (cliff, acceleration), board, veto, drag/tag, ROFR, co-sale, redemption, pay-to-play, conversion, dividends.
- **Convertibles & SAFE**: cap, discount, interest, maturity, MFN.

## 5. Governance, Monitoring, Exits
- **Governance**: board composition, veto, reporting, information rights.
- **Monitoring**: KPIs, milestones, reporting.
- **Exits**: M&A, IPO, secondary, buyback, write-off; timing; waterfall.
- **Zombie companies**, down rounds.

## 6. Exam Patterns (2023–2025)
- Valuation calculations (pre/post, ownership, dilution).
- Multi-round cap table.
- Term sheet clause identification & negotiation.
- GoStage down round case.
- Conceptual: VC vs PE, power law, staged financing, governance.
- Exit waterfall with liquidation preference.

## 7. Study Strategy
- Master T3 & T4 first (calculations + term sheet = exam core).
- Then T5 (exit/governance), T2 (evaluation), T1 (ecosystem).
- Practice with 2023–2025 exam solutions.
